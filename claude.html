We tried to cheat

Two ways to rewrite an AI's history. Both caught.

Most AI logs are trusted because the company holds them. Here's what happens when the company holding the keys tries to tidy up the record.

Hide a failure, re-sign with the real key

We deleted one bounce to make the outreach look cleaner, then re-signed the whole history with EDEN's own key. The signature checks out on its own (OK). Against the seals the witness already holds, it fails, and the witness refuses the rewritten seal on arrival.

vs witness seals → BAD_CONSISTENCY · witness on arrival → FORK_DETECTED

Change who approved an email

We edited one record to remove the human approval, without re-signing. The record no longer matches the sealed tree, so it's caught straight away.

result → BAD_ROOT

Honest notes
  • This log was sealed on 26 October from Gmail's sent records, after the emails were sent. A production integration seals each action as it happens.
  • Entries fingerprint the recipient and subject, not the message body. The Gmail message id lets the account holder produce the exact message.
  • For this demonstration the witness is run by EDEN. In production it's an independent party, such as an insurer, an auditor or a customer.
