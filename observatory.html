    What you are looking at

    Real sky, real time

    Satellites: positions calculated in your browser right now from public orbit data (CelesTrak), refreshed every 6 hours, using the standard SGP4 model. Accurate to a few kilometres; this is a calculation, not live tracking. Satellites are drawn as dots and icons, not to scale.

    Stars and constellations: real positions of 5,044 stars visible to the eye, with the IAU constellation figures and borders (d3-celestial, BSD licence).

    Earth: maps based on NASA imagery, lit by the Sun where it is right now. Clouds are a fixed picture, not today's weather.

    Your orbit: real two-body gravity. You orbit Earth like a satellite does.

    Your logbook: a real EDEN record. Sightings are hashed into a Merkle tree (RFC 6962) and sealed with an Ed25519 signature, all on your device. The observing is a game; the seal is real cryptography.

    Credits: CelesTrak (orbits), d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn (stars, BSD-3), satellite.js (MIT), Solar System Scope (Earth maps, CC BY 4.0) and three.js examples, based on NASA imagery.
