What this does not claim

  • That a decision was right: EDEN proves what was decided. Correctness needs targeted re-runs.
  • That an input fingerprint matches reality: a lying sensor produces sealed lies.
  • Wall-clock time on its own: a witness receipt bounds it by the witness's clock.
  • Independent review: both implementations share an author; an outside review is the next milestone.
  • Field deployment: robots, agents and data sets here are simulated; overheads and every verification are measured.
  • Currency: RSU is a capacity receipt, not money.
